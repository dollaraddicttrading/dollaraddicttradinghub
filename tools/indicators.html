Indicators & Chart Tools

A simple checklist for interpreting charts consistently.

Range High / Low

Mark the high and low of a completed candle or session range. Do not move the levels retrospectively to fit the outcome.

Liquidity Sweep

Observe whether price trades beyond a prior high/low and then closes back within the reference range. Confirm with your own rules.

Spread & Risk

Check spread, lot size, stop distance and the possible account-currency loss before placing a trade.

This page provides educational descriptions, not personalized trade recommendations.
