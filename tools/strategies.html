Trading Strategies

Educational strategy notes, not signals or financial advice.

CRT Range Sweep

Mark a completed higher-timeframe range, watch for a liquidity sweep, then look for price to return inside the range. Define invalidation and risk before entry.

Session Context

Consider session timing, scheduled news, spreads and volatility. Session clocks are indicative and should not replace broker specifications.

Trade Review

Log the setup, entry, exit, result and lesson in the journal. Review a meaningful sample before changing rules.

No strategy guarantees profits. Backtest and demo-test before risking money.
