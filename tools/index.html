DOLLARADDICT Academy

Short lessons to support consistent process and risk awareness.

Lesson 1 · Position sizing

Choose the amount you are willing to lose first. Calculate position size from the entry-to-stop distance and broker-specific contract value, then consider fees and slippage.

  • Define the invalidation level before entering.
  • Do not widen a stop just to keep a trade open.
  • Keep a written record of why you took the setup.
Lesson 2 · CRT range and liquidity sweep

Define the range using a consistent candle and timeframe. A sweep beyond a high or low followed by a close back inside may fit a rule set, but it can fail and should be tested against historical data.

Lesson 3 · Journal review

Review samples large enough to be meaningful. Track net results, average win/loss, drawdown, execution quality and whether rules were followed—not win rate alone.

Lesson 4 · Backtesting responsibly

Use clean historical data, document assumptions, avoid look-ahead bias and account for spread, commission, slippage and data quality. Test out-of-sample before considering any live deployment.

Lesson 5 · News and discipline

High-impact releases can cause fast price moves, wider spreads and slippage. Decide in advance whether you will trade, reduce risk or stay flat around events.

Lesson 6 · Protect your account

Trading can result in losses, including losses larger than expected during gaps or volatile conditions. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
